# Profile ideas — for tomorrow's brainstorm

You said banners, colours and favourites are what everyone does. Here is what's **already built** and what
could come next. Pick the ones that excite you; I'll build them.

## Built tonight

- **Hot takes** — the titles where your score is furthest from the community average, auto-written
  ("Underrated, apparently" / "Not buying the hype"). Nobody has to write anything; it's instantly shareable.
- **Anime years** — a bar per release year of what you've watched: your anime life at a glance.
- **Taste DNA** — six directions the model discovered, with your position on each.
- **Taste match** — a visitor who's logged in sees "82% taste match", the shows you'd argue about, and the
  ones you both love.
- **Share card** — a 1200×630 image (fanned favourite covers, stats) that is also the link preview.
- **Section order and visibility**, colour, banner, bio.

## Built later (Customise page)

- Nine colours (four seasons plus matcha, violet, crimson, paper), four header styles (soft art, poster, solid ink,
  plain), banner focus, picture (initial, top favourite, none), poster or quiet name, fanned covers on or off.
- Pronouns, location, up to four links.
- Favourites: own heading, three layouts (number one big, top three big, all equal).
- **Hill I'll die on** (idea 6, without the visitor voting), **Watching now** strip, **Drop wall** (idea 7, off by default).
- Scores as 8.7, 87 or hidden (hidden scores are removed server-side), chart grouped by season, score or one wall,
  which lists visitors can open, and which one opens first. Each number under the name can be hidden.
- A live phone-sized preview next to the controls on wide screens.

## Ideas — new kinds of profile content

1. **Taste twin** — "the anime character / studio / director whose taste you most resemble", from the
   embedding space (e.g. you're 91% Madhouse). Very shareable.
2. **Konomi knows me** — the model's live track record on your profile: "guessed my score within 1 point
   78% of the time". A badge that grows as you rate.
3. **Your constellation** — your rated titles drawn as a small star chart (from the galaxy) with lines between
   the ones you love most. Every profile gets a unique, recognisable shape; could be the avatar.
4. **Gateway anime** — the first title you rated highest from each era; "the show that got me into it".
5. **Seasonal report card** — per season you've watched: what you watched, what you dropped, your MVP.
   Looks like a seasonal chart poster you can export.
6. **Hill I'll die on** — pin one hot take with a one-line reason. Visitors can tap "agree / fight me".
7. **Drop wall** — the shows you dropped, struck through, with the episode you quit at. Funny and honest.
8. **Recommend to me** — visitors can suggest one anime; Konomi shows its predicted score for you next to it,
   so friends see whether their rec will land.
9. **Blind-pick challenge** — a friend opens your profile and gets 5 "which did they enjoy more?" duels about
   *your* list; their score = how well they know your taste. (Blindlap energy.)
10. **Mood shelves** — auto-clusters of your list from the taste model ("your melancholy sci-fi corner",
    "comfort slice-of-life"), named by the cluster tags.

## Ideas — how it looks

- **Profile as a poster**: one tall, printable seasonal-chart poster of your top 30, in your colour —
  downloadable as a phone wallpaper.
- **Living header**: the banner art slowly pans; your favourite's cover colour tints the whole page.
- **Compare mode**: open two profiles side by side, with the shared titles lined up between them.
